Support Escalation Policy

Priority P1 incidents are production issues that prevent the core service from functioning for a customer.

P1 incidents should be escalated immediately to the on-call engineering team.

Priority P2 incidents significantly affect functionality but have an available workaround.

P2 incidents should normally be reviewed by the support lead during business hours.

Priority P3 requests are normal questions, minor issues, or feature requests and follow the standard support queue.
